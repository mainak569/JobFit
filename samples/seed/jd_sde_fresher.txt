About the role
We are hiring 2026 graduates into our engineering teams in Hyderabad and Pune. After a
six-week bootcamp you will join a product team working on web, backend or platform
services, with mentoring from senior engineers.

What you will do
- Write clean, well-tested code in Java, C++ or Python.
- Build and maintain features in web applications and backend services.
- Work with SQL databases such as MySQL or PostgreSQL.
- Debug issues, write unit tests and participate in code reviews.
- Learn how production systems are designed, deployed and monitored.
- Work in an Agile team with regular sprints and demos.

Requirements
- B.Tech / B.E. / M.Tech in Computer Science or a related branch, graduating in 2026.
- Strong fundamentals in data structures and algorithms.
- Good understanding of OOP, DBMS, operating systems and computer networks.
- Proficiency in at least one of Java, C++ or Python.
- Strong problem solving skills; competitive programming experience is a plus.
- Basic knowledge of Git and Linux.

Nice to have
- Internship or project experience with web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React).
- Exposure to cloud platforms such as AWS or Azure.
- Understanding of REST APIs.
